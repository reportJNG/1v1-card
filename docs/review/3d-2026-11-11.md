## Budgets

- **Eggs:** 3,040-7,420 triangles including the outline hull (base egg 1,520). One toon colour set
  per egg; a baked 512 px texture at export.
- **Fighters:** 7,672-8,640 triangles including outlines (budget 12,000). The body is Roblox's
  built-in R15 with BodyColors, so only the identity parts (hair, coat, collar or scarf, chains,
  armour, weapon) would be uploaded.
- **Guardian:** 5,028 triangles at 1.8x scale; its world colour is a runtime tint of the glowing
  seams.
